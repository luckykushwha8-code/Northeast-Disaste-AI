Field Evacuation & Direct Life-Safety Protocols

Mandatory ASDMA Directive
1

High Ground Transit

Relocate immediately to designated multi-purpose flood shelters or highland embankments. Never navigate fast-running currents above ankle depth.

2

Livestock Safety

Untether cattle and draft animals. Move livestock to ASDMA elevated highlands (raised earthen mounds) with reserve dry fodder.

3

Water Hygiene

Deploy Halazone / Chlorine purification tablets into all stored water. Seal tube-well heads with waterproof sheeting against cross-contamination.
